# Pure path products and the remaining source restriction

**Status: independently audited paper induction with external exact finite
base certificates.** This concerns the larger space of all state-path products,
not yet the particular combinations generated by N. No source independence
claim follows from this note. The path expansion and its normalization are
B38–B42 of `SOURCE24_BINARY_QUOTIENT.md`.

Write

\[
 f_{ta}=X_a\prod_{b\ne a}L_{tb},\qquad D_t=\prod_aL_{ta},\qquad
 V_m=\operatorname{span}\left\{\prod_{t=1}^m f_{t,a_t}\right\}.
 \tag{PS1}
\]

Let Z_t be the six points consisting of the three same-block line
intersections and the three matching intersections L_ta=X_a=0. Let
Z_[m] be their union and I(Z_[m])_d its degree-d homogeneous vanishing
space. Every product in PS1 belongs to I(Z_[m])_(3m).

Assume the explicit local line and six-point conditions used in
`CubicIdeal.lean`, and cross-nonincidence: no line of a different block
passes through any Z_t point. These are geometric nonvanishing conditions,
not rank assumptions on the econometric source family.

## One degree less in the interpolation step

Suppose every pair of blocks admits interpolation at its twelve points
by homogeneous quartics. For q>=2 and a target point P in Z_t, choose a
second block u. A quartic separator s_P for Z_t union Z_u gives

\[
 S_P=s_P\prod_{v\ne t,u}D_v,\qquad \deg S_P=3q-2. \tag{PS2}
\]

It vanishes at all other points of Z_[q]. At P its value is nonzero,
because the separator is nonzero and cross-nonincidence makes every
remaining D_v nonzero. Dividing by this scalar gives a full separating
family. Thus evaluation at the 6q old points is surjective already in
degree 3q−2.

The existing Lean theorem
`BlockExtension.exists_corrected_cubic_decomposition` applies to any
interpolation degree k. Its input has degree k+5, and its three corrected
cubic coefficients have degree k+2 and vanish at every old point.
Take k=3q−2. For F in I(Z_[q+1])_(3q+3), this yields

\[
 F=\sum_{a=0}^2 f_{q+1,a}c_a,\qquad
 c_a\in I(Z_{[q]})_{3q}. \tag{PS3}
\]

Consequently, a base equality V_4=I(Z_[4])_12 propagates by induction:

\[
 V_m=I(Z_{[m]})_{3m}\quad(m\ge4). \tag{PS4}
\]

This is a new degree-3m statement. The existing `CubicProducts` theorem
has an additional coordinate factor and concerns degree 3m+1; its result
cannot simply be substituted for PS4.

## Normalized paths and generic quantifiers

Let D=prod_t D_t and assume D(e2) is nonzero. The polynomial D belongs
to I(Z_[m])_(3m). The map

\[
 \Pi(p)=p-\frac{p(e_2)}{D(e_2)}D \tag{PS5}
\]

projects this space onto its subspace vanishing also at e2, with kernel
span(D). Of the pure products only the all-two path is nonzero at e2,
and its value divided by D(e2) equals 1/prod_t A_t(2,2). Therefore PS5
maps the pure products exactly to the normalized cleared paths in B42.
Once PS4 holds, their span is the entire extra-point vanishing space,
of dimension

\[
 \binom{3m+2}{2}-6m-1=9\binom m2+3m. \tag{PS6}
\]

The 6m point conditions are independent by PS2 and degree elevation.
The condition at e2 adds one more, since D vanishes on all old points
and is nonzero at e2.

This extra-point vanishing space is precisely the cleared component-two
ambient space. Indeed D R2/X2 for R in W0 vanishes at same-block line
intersections by the absence of those double residues, at matching points
by the departure factor, and at e2 by vertex vanishing. Its dimension is
r+3m by the already proved kernel and surjectivity statement B23. The
inclusion and the equal dimensions in PS6 therefore give equality.

For generic validity on the common-G family, it suffices to certify one
nonzero two-block quartic evaluation minor of size 12 and one nonzero
four-block pure-product minor of size 67, with the required geometry.
After clearing any denominators these are nonzero polynomials in the
shared G entries and independent time factors. Relabel the pair minor
for each required pair and multiply these polynomials, the four-block
base minor, and the geometric nonvanishing factors. The parameter ring
is an integral domain, so their product remains nonzero. The two base
certificates may even use different common-G witnesses.

This gives a nonempty generic open for each fixed finite m; positivity
can be imposed because a nonzero real polynomial cannot vanish on the
whole positive orthant. It does not assert that every fixed G works or
describe one fixed finite open valid simultaneously for unbounded m.

## Why this does not yet prove spanning by N

The actual N generators select particular parameter-dependent linear
combinations of the paths. Full spanning by all paths does not imply
full spanning by those combinations.

An exact append formula identifies the issue. For an old formal generator
combination retain both R=I−Q and its prefix I. Write
p_d(R)=D R_d/X_d. If a new block is appended to its completed words, then

\[
 R'=I-QK_*=RK_*+I(I-K_*),
\]
\[
 \frac{DD_*R'_2}{X_2}
 =\sum_dA_*(d,2)f_{*,d}p_d(R-I)+D_*p_2(I). \tag{PS7}
\]

For first-slot differences, I=0. For selected output-zero sources,
I=X_aK_t(a,0)e0 and the correction to the transfer of R is
−A_*(0,2)f_*,0 A_t(a,0)X_aD/L_ta. For long-empty rows, I=X_a e_a,
and that correction is delta_(a,2)DD_*−A_*(a,2)f_*,a D.

Since R is zero-sum, sum_d X_d p_d(R)=0. The transferred part can be
written as g_1 p_1(R)+g_2 p_2(R), where

\[
 g_j=X_jL_{*,3-j}
       \bigl(A_*(j,2)L_{*,0}-A_*(0,2)L_{*,j}\bigr),\quad j=1,2.
 \tag{PS8}
\]

Thus knowing that p_2(N) fills its scalar space does not control the
accompanying p_1 component or the prefix correction. A proof must show
how the actual new generators cancel those terms, or establish a different
surjective transfer. Append is naturally defined on formal coefficients:
an old rational relation R=0 can still append to I(I−K_*) if its prefix
is nonzero. Keeping (R,I) avoids silently assuming a well-defined map on
the old rational space.
